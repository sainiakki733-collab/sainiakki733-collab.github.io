Privacy Policy for Titan Memory Match

Effective date: 30 August 2026

Titan Memory Match stores the birth date entered in its neutral age screen, game progress, best scores, and sound preferences locally on the device. No account is required, and the developer does not operate a server that receives this local data.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share an IP address (which may estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform displays a consent message before ads are requested. Eligible users can reopen Privacy choices inside the app. Users can reset or delete their advertising ID in Android settings. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Age-appropriate treatment

Titan Memory Match uses a neutral birth-date screen to choose child, teen, or adult advertising treatment. The entered birth date stays on the device and is not sent to the developer, Google, or advertisers. Only the resulting age-treatment signal is supplied to the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Child and teen users receive restricted, age-appropriate advertising treatment.

Retention and deletion

The locally stored birth date and game data remain until the user clears app storage or uninstalls. Advertising data is handled under Google's policies, age treatment, and the user's consent choices.
